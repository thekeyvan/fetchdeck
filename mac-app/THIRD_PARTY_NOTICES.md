# Third-Party Software

FetchDeck includes the following command-line components:

- **yt-dlp 2026.07.04** — The Unlicense. Source:
  <https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/tree/2026.07.04>
- **FFmpeg 8.1.2** — built from official source without nonfree components and
  distributed under the LGPL. Its exact source archive is included with this
  notice. Upstream source: <https://ffmpeg.org/releases/>
- **LAME 3.101** — linked to provide MP3 encoding and distributed under the
  LGPL. Its exact source archive is included with this notice. Upstream source:
  <https://sourceforge.net/projects/lame/files/lame/3.101/>
- **Node.js 24.18.0 LTS** — distributed under the terms in the bundled Node.js
  license file. Source: <https://github.com/nodejs/node/tree/v24.18.0>

The complete yt-dlp third-party license inventory and full license texts for the
bundled runtimes are included beside this notice in the app’s Resources folder.
The FFmpeg/LAME build script is included there as the reproducible build recipe.
